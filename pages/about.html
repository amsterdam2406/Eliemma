Who We Are

A Team Built on Care, Science & Leadership

At EliEmma Health, we believe that quality healthcare begins with dignity, compassion, and personalized attention. Our mission is to help individuals maintain their independence and quality of life by providing professional home healthcare services.

Founded with a passion for serving families and communities, we are committed to delivering reliable, patient-centered care that supports physical, emotional, and social well-being — bridging gaps through a seamless, coordinated approach to health and wellbeing.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional home healthcare services that empower clients to live safely, comfortably, and independently while giving families peace of mind.

Our Vision

To become a trusted leader in home healthcare by setting the standard for compassionate care, clinical excellence, and meaningful client relationships.

Ganiat Mumuney, MMI

Executive Officer

With a lifelong commitment to improving the health and well-being of others, Ganiat brings a unique blend of compassionate caregiving, scientific expertise, and strategic leadership to our team.

Read full biography

Her career began on the front lines of patient care, supporting aging populations as a nursing assistant and providing personal care for hospice and quadriplegic patients. These early experiences shaped her deep understanding of the human side of healthcare and instilled a passion for enhancing quality of life for patients and families.

Driven by a desire to better understand the factors that influence health outcomes, she transitioned into research, studying the effects of food and drug interactions and investigating the role of food chemistry in birth defects. This scientific foundation expanded her perspective on preventive health and the broader impact of healthcare innovation.

Throughout her career, one principle has remained constant: a dedication to making life better for people and communities. This mission led her into pharmaceutical development and clinical research, where she supported the advancement of new therapies and contributed to clinical safety reviews, operational excellence, and strategic initiatives. Over time, she progressed into leadership roles, guiding teams and driving organizational strategy and operations.

She holds a Master of Science in Medical Informatics from Northwestern University and a Bachelor of Science in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from the University of New Mexico. Her diverse experience across patient care, research, drug development, informatics, and healthcare leadership provides a well-rounded perspective that continues to shape meaningful improvements in healthcare delivery and patient outcomes.
